# AceHire AI 🚀

**AceHire AI** is an AI-powered interview preparation and resume optimization platform designed to help job seekers prepare strategically for specific roles. Instead of providing generic interview questions, AceHire AI analyzes a candidate's profile and target job description to generate a personalized preparation roadmap, identify skill gaps, and create a tailored resume.

---

## 📌 Overview

Preparing for interviews often involves using generic resources that may not align with a specific role or company. AceHire AI solves this problem by creating a customized interview preparation experience based on:

- Your existing resume
- The job description of the role you're targeting
- A brief self-description

Using this information, the platform generates role-specific interview guidance and actionable insights to improve your chances of success.

---

## ✨ Features

### 📄 Resume Analysis
Upload your current resume to provide the platform with your existing skills, experience, and qualifications.

### 🎯 Job Description-Based Preparation
Paste the job description of the role you're targeting, allowing AceHire AI to tailor recommendations specifically to that position.

### 👤 Personalized Candidate Profile
Provide a self-description so the platform can better understand your background and career goals.

### 💻 Technical Interview Questions
Generates technical questions relevant to the target role and required skills.

### 🤝 Behavioral Interview Questions
Creates behavioral interview questions designed to assess soft skills and workplace readiness.

### 🧠 Question Intent Analysis
Every interview question includes an explanation of what the interviewer is actually trying to evaluate.

### ✅ Model Answers
Provides sample high-quality answers to help candidates understand how to structure effective responses.

### 📊 Job Match Score
Calculates how closely a candidate's profile aligns with the target role.

### 🔍 Skill Gap Analysis
Identifies missing or weak skills compared to the job requirements.

### 🛣️ Personalized Roadmap
Generates a preparation roadmap highlighting areas for improvement and recommended learning paths.

### ⭐ AI Resume Optimization
The platform's standout feature.

Using:
- The uploaded resume
- The target job description

AceHire AI generates a new, role-specific resume that optimizes existing experience and skills to better match the desired position.

2. Paste the target job description.

   - Technical Questions
